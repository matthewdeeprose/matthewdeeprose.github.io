# Slide number: 4
What is the assessment?
AbilityNet developed the model with Alistair McNaught and still runs it.

The institutional framework reviews evidence of inclusive practice university-wide.

We can earn a bronze, silver, or gold badge from the evidence.

It supports the Steering Group's commitment to benchmark our digital accessibility.

It feeds the Digital Strategic Plan and the next disability action plan.

## What the model measures (1)

1 of 2
Strategy and structure

Course-level accessibility

# Slide number: 8
## The process

Benchmarking session
2.5 hours, all stakeholders
AbilityNet assesses our current position and builds a roadmap report.
Catch-up meeting
About 2 hours, main stakeholders
Six months after the Benchmarking Session, we review progress against the roadmap.
Review and badging
About 2 hours, main stakeholders
Six months after that, AbilityNet rescores our evidence and confirms our badge.
AbilityNet surveys stakeholders two to three weeks before the Benchmarking Session. The survey takes about 20 minutes.

# Slide number: 9
The stakeholder list
We grouped stakeholders into 20 areas, from teaching and learning to procurement.

Each group names a suggested main stakeholder, who takes the full survey.

Main stakeholders join every session; other stakeholders join the Benchmarking Session.

Two groups on the list already represent your networks directly.
