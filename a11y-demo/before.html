What a screen-reader user hits here

  • The Full name box has no connected label, so a screen reader just says “edit text” — you cannot tell what to type.
  • The ? help control is not a real button: keyboard users cannot reach it, and a screen reader does not announce it as something you can press.
  • The campus options are read out as “Highfield, Avenue, Boldrewood” with no heading — you never hear that you are choosing a campus.
  • If your email is wrong, the only sign is a red outline. A screen reader announces nothing, and the message itself is too faint to read.
  • The Register button is really just coloured text — you cannot reach or press it with the keyboard.
  • The page does not declare its language, so a screen reader may use the wrong voice and mispronounce words.
