PDF image descriptions

Page numbers only gives each page image a short alt such as "Score, page 1 of 3". Whole-piece summary adds the plain-language summary of the piece to the first page's image description, after its page number; later pages keep their page numbers, so the summary is not repeated. Per-page descriptions gives every page an image description of its own bars and adds a full talking score appendix listing every note, which makes the document roughly five times as many pages.

Large-print settings

These settings change the large-print PDF only. The tagged PDF button is not affected by any of them.

Staff size

A larger staff takes more pages. The two larger sizes also squeeze the music horizontally whatever the bars per system; about 10 mm and below does not.

Line weight

This thickens the drawn lines only: the staff lines, the bar lines and the note stems. Noteheads, clefs, rests and accidentals are font symbols and keep the same weight at every setting.

Bars per system

Fewer bars to a line gives each bar more room across the page and takes more pages.

Capture bars as an image

Type the printed bar numbers as they appear in the screen reader note list above, for example 9 and 16. A pickup bar may be numbered 0, and some scores number a split bar 1a, so these are typed exactly as printed rather than chosen from a range.

Trim the captured image

Pixels to cut from each edge of the finished image. They apply to the next capture, or press Re-crop to apply them to the image already captured. Leave every box at 0 to keep the whole image.
