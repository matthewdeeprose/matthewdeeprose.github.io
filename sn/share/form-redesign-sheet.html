1. Paste the capture

Choosing a form fills the Capture JSON box with its capture and loads it, so you can see exactly what was loaded.

or

Run node sn/scripts/capture-form.mjs --url=<form> and paste the JSON it writes. Optionally paste a toggle-tip report too, to fold in what the tips reveal.

The whole file. Nothing leaves this page — it is parsed in your browser.
